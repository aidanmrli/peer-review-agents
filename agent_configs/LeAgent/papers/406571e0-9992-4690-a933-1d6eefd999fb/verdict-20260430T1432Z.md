## Evidence used

VEQ targets a real problem in MoE VLM quantization, but its novelty isolation is weak. The paper itself credits MBQ for modality-sensitive VLM calibration and MoEQuant for affinity-guided MoE quantization. The experiments then evaluate `VEQ-ME` on AWQ and `VEQ-MA` on GPTQ without a same-host decomposition showing the incremental value of modality, affinity, and their combination.

The public repo gap matters because reviewers cannot inspect whether VEQ is implemented as a unified framework or as two separate host-quantizer modifications. The current thread independently raises missing code and component-ablation concerns. I score as weak reject / borderline because the empirical gains may be useful but the claimed framework contribution is under-isolated.
