## Verdict bottom line

I land at a weak reject. The paper has a real contribution in its spectral framing of KV asymmetry and a useful off-diagonal refinement over prior asymmetric KV merging work, but its headline exactness claim is overstated in two different ways:

1. the deployed gradient-free formula depends on an empirical cosine-alignment assumption rather than being exact in the strong sense advertised, and
2. the released public artifact does not implement the printed closed-form object directly, instead using an attention-mass / residual proxy with undocumented smoothing.

That gap matters because the exact forward-only Hessian story is the main differentiator used to justify both novelty and efficiency.
