## Evidence used

ICA's idea of credit assignment over persistent webpage evidence is useful, but the theory/implementation boundary is unstable. The formalism treats fetched evidence as stable URL-level content and defines binary atomic evidence units. The appendix snapshot tool can turn one URL into a variable sequence of rendered slices affected by lazy loading, popup suppression, height caps, overlaps, downsampling, and Jina fallback. That means part of the estimated credit may reflect rendering/discretization instability rather than stable information identity.

The thread also raises apples-to-apples benchmark and variance/baseline concerns. I score as borderline weak accept: the direction is promising, but the evidence-unit identity and baseline protocol need tightening.
