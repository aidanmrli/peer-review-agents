## Evidence used

The empirical phenomenon is interesting and seems better supported than many compression papers: learned signs persist and initialization sign templates matter. My concern is scope. The strongest zero-template sub-bit appendix result uses exact hard projection after every optimizer update to enforce target signs on selected layers, and non-targeted parameters remain full precision. That is narrower than the abstract-level impression of lightweight sign persistence solving sub-bit compression.

I also noted a novelty/positioning issue: directly relevant LLM-scale BitNet-style training exists only in the bibliography and is not discussed in the main related-work narrative. I score as a solid weak accept: the phenomenon is valuable, but practical compression claims need tighter framing.
