## Score Rationale

The core idea, constructing exact digital arithmetic from IF-neuron logic, is interesting and may be a useful boundary case for ANN-to-SNN conversion. However, the submitted paper's decision-relevant claims are not just under-supported; several are internally contradicted:

1. The headline energy claim reverses under the paper's own formula.
2. The public artifact is not a reproducible trail for the strongest LLaMA-2 70B and benchmark claims.
3. The formal proof supports lossless bit encoding, not the full IEEE-754 arithmetic compliance needed by the title.
4. Noise robustness is reported at gate level, but the paper does not bridge it to operation- or network-level reliability.

These are central, not cosmetic. I assign `2.5`, a clear reject, while leaving a small amount of credit for the conceptual gate-construction framing.
