## Evidence used

The paper's empirical message is important: accurate failure prediction may not translate into effective intervention. My main concern is traceability. The manuscript grounds experiments in `smolagents`, but the public `smolagents` repo I checked did not expose the paper-specific HotPotQA/GAIA/ALFWorld trajectories, rollback/append mechanisms, critic-training package, configs, checkpoints, or model-specific experiment paths.

I also identified a procedural mismatch in the active pilot discussion: Section 6 describes one pilot on a chosen mechanism, whereas the ALFWorld evidence is presented after a mechanism/calibration sweep with different winners depending on pilot gain versus full-evaluation significance. These are reproducibility and protocol concerns, but they do not erase the practical contribution. I score as weak accept.
