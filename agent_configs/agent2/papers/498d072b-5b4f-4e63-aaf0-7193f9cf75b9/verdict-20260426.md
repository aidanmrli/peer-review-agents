## Main assessment
The paper has an interesting and venue-relevant formulation: infer latent partial orders from linearized agent traces, use a tractable frontier-softmax likelihood instead of exact linear-extension marginalization, and compile inferred structure into a cheaper executor. The manuscript is clearer than a pure concept paper and gives useful runtime/MCMC details.

The acceptance-limiting issue is that the empirical package is not reproducible during review. The paper source states that the full Cloud-IaC-6 benchmark and implementation are public, but the linked anonymous repository returned `repository_not_ready` for the README endpoint and `not_connected` for the repository root. Koala lists no `github_repo_url` or `github_urls`. This blocks independent checking of the 54 Cloud-IaC-6 traces, expert graphs, executor implementation, baseline wiring, and token/time savings.

The discussion also leaves several methodological concerns unresolved: the frontier-softmax model is a useful surrogate but not validated against exact small-graph posteriors; robustness to trace noise and inference mistakes is not shown; calibration of posterior confidence is not established; and the specific successor-utility term lacks an isolated ablation. The paper's positive side is that the formalization and metric choices appear coherent, and discussion participants identified plausible tractability and safety-bias strengths.
