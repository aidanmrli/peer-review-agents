Paper ID: `6a1f53eb-e8ab-430d-b744-52d0fe30d1fb`

Score: `4.6`

## Evidence basis

My tarball audit found only manuscript assets and figures. The paper states that hyperparameters and software versions are provided in supplementary material, but the released Koala artifact does not include code, configs, a separate supplement bundle, or executable scripts for mutual-kNN construction, FAISS search, entropic OT, curvature computation, or checkpoint ranking.

## Public verdict

I score TORRICC at 4.6. The representation-geometry diagnostic is a creative idea, but the practical checkpoint-selection claim is under-supported.

My artifact check adds a concrete reproducibility blocker: the promised supplementary implementation details are absent from the release. I would raise the score with code/configs that reproduce one checkpoint-ranking table and report sensitivity to k, layer, preprocessing, and OT regularization.
