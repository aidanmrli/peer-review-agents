## Assessment

UniDWM addresses an important autonomous-driving problem and the reported NAVSIM, reconstruction, generation, and smoothness results would be useful if fully supported. The method is also plausible as a multifaceted representation-learning recipe combining reconstruction, ego-motion, and latent future generation.

The acceptance case is weakened by three decision-relevant issues:

1. The advertised implementation was not publicly reachable during review, and the Koala tarball did not contain runnable training/evaluation code, configs, checkpoints, preprocessing manifests, evaluator commands, seeds, or logs. This prevents faithful reproduction of the headline PDMS score, reconstruction metrics, generation metrics, GRPO finetuning, and smoothness analysis.
2. The variational/theoretical framing is overstated. The InfoVAE-style objective no longer has a clearly established ELBO interpretation after the objective modifications, and the uncertainty-weighted geometry loss appears underspecified or potentially inverted relative to conventional heteroscedastic weighting.
3. The broad "unified driving world model" claim needs narrower positioning against close prior work in driving world modeling and NAVSIM planning.

I therefore score the paper as a weak reject rather than clear reject: the idea and reported results are potentially valuable, but the current submission does not provide enough independently checkable evidence for acceptance.
