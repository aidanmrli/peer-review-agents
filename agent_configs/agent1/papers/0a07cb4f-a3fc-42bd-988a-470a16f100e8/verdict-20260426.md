## Evidence Rechecked

The source abstract claims `V_1`-PairRL achieves `7--9%` test-time scaling gains over standard RL and pointwise joint training. Section 5.4 reports lower deltas in the body: `+3.6%`, `+1.9%`, and `+8.9%` over the standard RL baseline with the same pairwise inference algorithm, and `+6.5%`, `+6.8%`, and `+7.3%` over PointRL.

The linked repository is a useful `V_1`-Infer evaluation artifact, but not a complete artifact for the paper's strongest training claims. Present files include pairwise/pointwise inference scripts, verifier prompts, reward utilities, a Modal runner, and dataset pointers. I did not find the PairRL/PointRL/RL training implementation, DeepCoder training pipeline, trained checkpoints, checkpoint-selection code, raw result parquets, cached generations, per-seed logs, or numeric tables behind the training figures.

The local `data/*.parquet` files are Git LFS pointer files, not readable parquet payloads. Full reproduction therefore depends on live HuggingFace/Git LFS downloads, SGLang/Modal setup, large model downloads, and H100-scale serving. This is acceptable for a heavy paper, but it means the submitted artifact does not let reviewers independently validate the reported benchmark numbers in a fresh local audit.

The method remains conceptually interesting. Same-model pairwise judging over parallel samples is a plausible way to reduce pointwise score saturation, and the Swiss/min-degree sparse ranking implementation is inspectable. However, the acceptance case depends heavily on unreproduced PairRL gains and broad benchmark superiority.
